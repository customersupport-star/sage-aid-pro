# KJ Gilbertson CPA Website

## Goal
Build a polished, responsive one-page website for KJ Gilbertson CPA, LLC that feels established, trustworthy, elegant, and tailored to small business owners.

## What I’ll build
- Sticky, compact-on-scroll navigation with the supplied logo, desktop links, consultation button, and mobile menu.
- Full-width cinematic hero with dark overlay, requested messaging, two actions, trust line, and scroll indicator.
- Value proposition, four service cards, CPA partnership split section, small-business focus, tax-planning banner, four-step process, clearly labeled testimonial placeholders, FAQ accordion, final call-to-action, contact form, and minimal footer.
- Contact form validation and an on-page confirmation state; placeholder phone, email, address, and hours will remain clearly identified until real details are supplied.
- Responsive layouts for desktop through mobile, restrained scroll reveals, accessible controls, and reduced-motion support.
- Route-specific title, description, social metadata, semantic headings, descriptive alt text, and lazy-loaded supporting images.

## Visual direction
- Deep navy and charcoal foundations with warm off-white sections, slate/sage support colors, and restrained champagne-gold details.
- Elegant serif display type paired with a highly readable sans serif.
- Authentic consultation and small-business financial-planning imagery; no cash, calculator close-ups, staged handshakes, orange, or red.
- Use the uploaded client logo prominently in the navigation and preserve its original proportions.

## Technical details
- Implement within the existing TanStack Start homepage and Tailwind v4 token system.
- Create app-hosted visual assets, including a short hero background video and professional supporting imagery.
- Keep the form front-end only because no delivery address or data-storage requirement was provided; it will validate inputs and show confirmation without sending or storing submissions.
- Verify the page at desktop and mobile sizes, including navigation, accordion, form completion, layout, and media loading.
