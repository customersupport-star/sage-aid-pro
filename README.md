# Gilbertson Clarity

Create a complete, modern, professional, elegant, and classic website for:

Business Name: KJ Gilbertson CPA, LLC
Industry: Accounting & Finance
Business Type: CPA / Accounting Firm
Primary Services: Tax Return Preparation, Tax Planning, Bookkeeping, Financial Statements
Primary Target Audience: Small Business Owners
Competitors: Local CPA Firms
Colors to Avoid: Orange and red

OVERALL DESIGN DIRECTION

Design the website to feel:

Professional

Trustworthy

Established

Elegant

Classic

Sophisticated

Clean

Financially credible

Approachable but premium

The website should immediately communicate that KJ Gilbertson CPA, LLC is a reliable financial partner for small business owners.

Avoid making the website look like a generic accounting template. The design should feel custom-built for a premium CPA firm.

Use a refined visual identity with plenty of white/negative space, strong typography, subtle borders, sophisticated spacing, elegant imagery, and restrained animations.

Recommended Color Palette

Use a sophisticated finance-oriented palette:

Deep Navy: #102A43

Dark Charcoal: #1F2933

Slate Blue: #486581

Soft Sage/Muted Green: #718096 or #6B8E7B

Warm Off-White: #F7F5F0

White: #FFFFFF

Subtle Champagne/Gold accent: #B89B5E

Do NOT use bright colors, orange, red, neon colors, excessive gradients, or overly playful visuals.

Gold should only be used sparingly for premium accents, icons, borders, or small highlights.

TYPOGRAPHY

Use a sophisticated combination such as:

Headings: Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, or another elegant serif

Body: Inter, Lato, or Source Sans 3

Headings should feel established and authoritative while body text remains extremely readable.

Use strong typography hierarchy throughout the website.

Classic + Modern + Trustworthy + Sophisticated + Human

"This is a professional accounting firm that understands small businesses and can be trusted with important financial matters."

Prioritize elegance and credibility over excessive visual effects.

Make every section purposeful, visually balanced, and conversion-focused.

The final website should feel eye-catching without being flashy, premium without being intimidating, and classic without looking outdated.
